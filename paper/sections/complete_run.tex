The neural models were trained on CPU, with two logical CPUs and four epochs per model, on a declared subset of 600 series. With this budget they are reference points, and we compare them only with each other. TimesNet scored 290,397 artificially hidden donor hours with a WAPE of 0.917 to 0.934 across the mechanisms. For TFT, the next-day WAPE on 5,407 matched tuning donor days falls from 0.451--0.453 with the visible history to 0.413--0.415 with the TimesNet-recovered history. The two tasks have different targets and units, and neither WAPE is comparable with the Phase~4 WAPE. We could reproduce the official baseline~\cite{dingdong2025baseline} only in part: the as-documented TimesNet run finished only after a recorded patch for a blocked network download, and the upstream TFT prediction step was not run.

The Kaplan--Meier implementation passed all 8 of its unit tests and built 207 pools. When a pool cannot support the requested percentile, the order is left unestimated rather than borrowed from another policy. Calibration used 513,276 residuals from the calibration window, and 0.061 of the calibration rows were corrected from a pool coarser than category by sales tier. Sorting removed every remaining quantile crossing, which makes the quantiles ordered but not necessarily calibrated. The order and cost arithmetic passed 10 hand-computed checks. The Phase~8 lock then fixed the policy names, cost settings, model and calibration files, planned comparisons, and bootstrap settings, all before Phase~9 read any final-week outcome.
