The neural models ran on CPU with two logical CPUs and four training epochs per model on a declared subset of 600 series, so they are budget-limited references that we compare only with each other. TimesNet scored 290,397 artificially hidden donor hours with WAPE 0.917 to 0.934 across the mechanisms; TFT's next-day WAPE on 5,407 matched tuning donor days falls from 0.451--0.453 with the visible history to 0.413--0.415 with the TimesNet-recovered history. The two tasks differ in target and unit, and neither is comparable with the Phase~4 WAPE. Our reproduction of the official baseline~\cite{dingdong2025baseline} is partial: the as-documented TimesNet run completed only after a recorded patch for a blocked network download, and the upstream TFT prediction step was not run.

The Kaplan--Meier policy passed all 8 of its unit tests and built 207 pools; when a pool cannot support the requested percentile, the order is left unestimated. Calibration used 513,276 calibration-window residuals, 0.061 of the calibration rows were corrected from a pool coarser than category by sales tier, and sorting removed every remaining quantile crossing, which guarantees ordered quantiles, not calibrated ones. All 10 hand-computed checks of the order and cost arithmetic passed, and the Phase~8 lock then fixed the policy names, cost settings, model and calibration files, planned comparisons, and bootstrap settings before Phase~9 read any final-week outcome.
