\section{Development Runs and Model Training}
\label{sec:scale}

Run~S searched hyperparameters fully on 5,000 series; run~P used a reduced search on all 50,000 series. Their overall donor shares are almost equal (0.5580 and 0.5573), and their simulator summaries agree to within 0.001: the masked share of eligible donor days is 0.460, 0.566, and 0.832 for A, B, and C in run~S. Run~P has a lower q50 tuning WAPE (0.3124--0.3161 against 0.3334--0.3361 over the three mechanisms) and a longer runtime, but the runs differ in search mode as well as size, so neither difference can be attributed to scale.

Run~E did not reuse run~S. It executed its own Phase~0--4 stage, with the full search, in the same kernel as its later phases. Its fingerprints of the selected series, the split, and the three simulator masks equal those of run~S, and so do its donor counts and q50 tuning WAPEs at full stored precision (0.333440, 0.336140, and 0.335759), consistent with a deterministic pipeline; timestamps, pandas versions, and file hashes differ, so we treat the runs as separate evidence. One Phase~0 check of run~E stayed open because it compares fingerprints with an earlier execution in the same output folder, and the first execution has none; the comparison with run~S is the one it would have made. A prerequisite audit then passed 10 checks: it re-derived the simulator masks and feature matrices from the raw panel with exact agreement, verified the hashes of 33 predecessor files, and confirmed that the calibration and final-week days were unused. Run~E's later phases ran in the reduced mode with 200 bootstrap replicates, and all results from here on come from its 5,000 series.
