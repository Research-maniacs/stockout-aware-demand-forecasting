Run~S searched hyperparameters fully on 5,000 series, while run~P used a reduced search on all 50,000. The two runs have almost the same donor share (0.5580 and 0.5573), and their simulator summaries agree to within 0.001; in run~S, for example, mechanisms A, B, and C mask 0.460, 0.566, and 0.832 of the eligible donor days. Run~P has a lower q50 tuning WAPE (0.3124--0.3161 against 0.3334--0.3361 over the three mechanisms) and took longer to run, but because the runs differ in search mode as well as in size, neither difference can be put down to scale.

Run~E did not reuse run~S. It ran Phases~0--4 again with the full search and reproduced run~S's selected series, split, simulator masks, donor counts, and q50 tuning WAPEs (0.333440, 0.336140, and 0.335759) exactly, as expected from a deterministic pipeline with the same data and seed. Before its later phases, an audit rebuilt the simulator masks and feature matrices from the raw panel, found exact agreement, checked the hashes of 33 saved files, and confirmed that the calibration and final-week days were still unused. Run~E's later phases used 200 bootstrap replicates, and all results from here on come from its 5,000 series.
